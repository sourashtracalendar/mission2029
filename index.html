Overview

To ensure lifetime printing of Sourashtra Calendar, SVAYA SOURASHTRA SVABHAAVAM is creating a permanent corpus fund. Each Location contributes Rs.20,000 to the corpus — this can be shared by few donors together. 10 free calendars will be delivered every year for that Location at its chosen address.

📲 After transferring the donation amount, please WhatsApp your Donor Name, Contact Number and Calendar Delivery Address.

You may choose delivery to your own address, or to your local Perumal Temple / Sabha address with their concurrence. One Location = 10 Calendars / Year.
