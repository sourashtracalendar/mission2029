Overview

To ensure lifetime printing of Sourashtra Calendar, SVAYA SOURASHTRA SVABHAAVAM is creating a permanent corpus fund. Our mission is to reach 50+ Sourashtra locations. Each location contributes Rs.20,000 to the corpus — this can be shared by few donors together. 10 free calendars will be delivered every year for that location at its chosen address.

📲 After transferring Rs.20,000 per location (shared allowed), please WhatsApp your Donor Name, Contact and Calendar Delivery Address.

Delivery can be to your address or to your local Perumal Temple / Sabha with their concurrence. One Location = 10 Calendars / Year.
